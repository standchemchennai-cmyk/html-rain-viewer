Map layers & data

Basemap: Esri World Topographic Map (real roads/places). Satellite: Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA), Himawari full-disk enhanced cloud-top imagery, with published frame timestamps (may have poorer detail at the western edge near Chennai). Radar: RainViewer (approximately 2 h archive, limited Indian coverage). Lightning: LightningMaps community overlay (live only; third-party access is not guaranteed).

The timeline is built only from satellite times that JMA actually publishes. The map is independent of the weather feeds. Lightning strikes are always LIVE, including while viewing historical satellite imagery. They do not correspond to the selected historic frame.
