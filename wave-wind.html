Reading this map

Click the map to inspect any location. The time slider controls map colours, arrows, selected-point forecasts and tide plot together. All times are India Standard Time (IST).

The map is a sparse sampled approximation of forecast grids, not official native-resolution model tiles. Wind arrows point towards the direction of travel; numerical direction follows meteorological “from” convention.

Tides use independent Open-Meteo sea-level forecasts including tidal effects. Coastal accuracy is limited; do not use for navigation.
